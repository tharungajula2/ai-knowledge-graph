## ELI5
Artificial Intelligence is like building a computer brain. 
- Regular programming is telling the computer: "If you see a red light, stop."
- Machine Learning is showing the computer 1,000 pictures of traffic lights and letting it figure out what red means.
- Deep Learning is doing this with many layers of virtual brain cells (neurons) so it can understand complex things.
- Generative AI is when the brain gets so good it can create its own stories or pictures instead of just labeling them.
- Agentic AI is giving that brain a body/hands, letting it plan tasks, use tools, and make decisions on its own.

## The Real Explanation
The family tree of AI represents a progression of abstraction and capability:
1. **Artificial Intelligence (AI)**: The broad domain of simulating intelligent behavior in computers via rules or algorithms.
2. **Machine Learning (ML)**: A subset of AI focused on statistical models that learn patterns from data without explicit instruction (e.g. linear regression, random forests).
3. **Deep Learning (DL)**: A subset of ML utilizing multi-layered artificial neural networks (deep networks) to automatically extract hierarchical features from raw data.
4. **Generative AI (GenAI)**: Deep learning models (like Transformers, Diffusion models) trained to generate new content (text, code, images) by estimating probability distributions over sequence spaces.
5. **Agentic AI**: Systems built on generative models that exhibit autonomy, using reasoning loops, internal memory state, and tools (APIs) to achieve multi-step goals.

## Why It Matters
Understanding this hierarchy keeps you from conflating standard ML regression tasks with agentic LLM loops, ensuring you select the correct tool for production engineering problems.
