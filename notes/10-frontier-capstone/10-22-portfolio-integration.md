## The Real Explanation
**The positioning shift.** AI Knowledge Graph's portfolio entry graduates from project-card to centerpiece: the two-sentence framing (from the essay's spine — a stranger's ninety seconds), the film embedded, the live links (the brain, the deployed v2 demo-mode, the repo, the essay), and the receipts row (the numbers that survive skimming: 228 nodes lit, the fine-tune deltas, zero-exfil suite, deploy time). The story timeline gets its new final beat — the arc your Full Story tells, compressed to a milestone entry. And the framing discipline that has governed every surface you've built: *what it proves* over *what it uses* — the stack list is a footnote; the transformation is the headline.

**The propagation protocol — your own law, executed.** (1) Regenerate a current project evidence note to the post-v2 reality (the graph lit, v2 shipped, M1–M5 status, honest known-limitations — the document's voice has always been verifiable-or-silent; keep it). (2) Propagate truth-file → portfolio copy → AI chat grounding (`ai-context.ts` regenerated so JARVIZ answers about AI Knowledge Graph from the new truth — the meta-moment: your portfolio's AI accurately describing the AI system you built) → resume bullets → LinkedIn (the ready-made copy section of the truth file, refreshed). (3) The sync audit: one pass across every surface hunting contradictions — dates, counts, claims — because a single stale "199 ghost nodes" line undoes a year of credibility discipline.

**The honesty inventory, one last time.** The what-to-admit list, post-v2: single-operator system (not multi-tenant — and you can whiteboard the scaling path, Vol 09 taught it); demo-mode for public access (real credentials never exposed — that's a feature, say so); deferred frontier items per the spec. The never-claim list updates too. Interviewers who probe find the floor solid; that's the entire strategy.
